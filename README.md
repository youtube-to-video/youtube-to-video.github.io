# Clip Notes

Searching youtube to video nearly always means one thing: an MP4 you can play offline. Here is what the free tools do, and the limits they state upfront.

**Read the full page:** https://youtube-to-video.github.io/

The TurboScribe converter is the least annoying of the free options: paste a link, choose MP4, download, with no account, no software and no ad wall. It suits someone saving a talk for a flight or archiving their own upload. It suits you less if you need a specific resolution, because the page does not offer a quality picker, and other tools do. Two limits are stated plainly rather than buried, and they are the reason to trust the rest: copyrighted music cannot be pulled through it, and creators can opt their videos out with a tag. If the download is really raw material for a video of your own, Kyncept generates clips from text or an image instead, which sidesteps the whole rights question.
